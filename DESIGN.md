# Blank Life — Anime Field Adventure

## Direction

Render the existing island as a playable 3D anime world: three-dimensional terrain, buildings, vehicles, and characters with hand-inked contours, stepped cel lighting, expressive faces, and a compact game HUD. The world should feel like an anime adventure that can be explored, not a flat anime poster placed behind the current interface.

## Scene

- Keep the island and its movement, destinations, career missions, online-player presence, phone, and laptop interactions.
- Use cel-shaded light bands and selective ink outlines on characters and architecture; preserve readable silhouettes at the normal camera distance.
- Use a coherent palette of deep ocean blue, forest jade, misted sky, sunlit sand, and restrained coral/cyan quest accents.
- Keep character proportions expressive and anime-influenced while maintaining distinct silhouettes and stable player identity.
- Preserve day, sunset, night, mist, and mobile rendering modes.

## Interface

- Keep the in-world Phone and Laptop as the player's primary interface, with existing touch and keyboard controls.
- Give device panels an illustrated game-menu quality that matches the island, without covering the play space or reducing text contrast.
- Preserve accessible focus, readable type, and mobile safe-area behavior.

## Avoid

Photorealistic/PBR rendering, flat 2D scenery in place of the 3D world, copied anime/game characters or logos, excessive bloom, and decorative panels that obscure movement or exploration.
